## Drum sounds: CrocellKit

* **CrocellKit** for DrumGizmo, version 1.1 (July 2018), as distributed by the DrumGizmo project: <https://drumgizmo.org>
  (kit page: <https://drumgizmo.org/wiki/doku.php?id=kits:crocellkit>).
* Sampled and edited by **Lars Muldjord**; kit set up by Andreas, drummer of Crocell (<https://crocell.dk>); microphones set up by
  **Jacob Olsen** of JBOSound (<https://jbosound.com>). The kit was recorded while the band Crocell was making their 2018 album "Relics".
* License: CC BY 4.0.
* **Changes made here:** from every instrument only the loudest sample layer is used; the two ambience channels are mixed to mono,
  resampled from 48 kHz to 44.1 kHz, trimmed to the hit, shortened with a smooth fade-out, normalized and saved as 16-bit WAV
  (to fit the pedal's 604 KB drum memory), then arranged into the slot order of Valeton's rhythm patterns (some sounds are used in two slots) and lowered in level to match Valeton's original samples. Instruments used: KDrumL, Snare, SnareRim, HihatClosed, HihatOpen, HihatPedal, Tom1, Tom2,
  FTom1, CrashL, RideR, RideRBell, SnareRimShot. Scripts: `tools/drumkit/make_samples.py`, `tools/drumkit/make_kit.py`.

The rhythm patterns are Valeton's own and are not part of this credit.
